## What you can do with PunctuOwlity

PunctuOwlity lets you track all of your upcoming events in one place. Every event card shows the day, date, title, and time at a glance — and a clear icon tells you whether a reminder is on or off.

## Get started in three steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [PunctuOwlity](https://apursley2012.github.io/punctuowlity) and click **Sign Up** to create your free account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your notification preference">
    Choose whether to receive SMS-style alerts. You can always change this later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your first event">
    Tap the **＋** button on the events screen, fill in the details, and save. Your event appears on the dashboard immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  PunctuOwlity stores all your data directly in your browser using local storage — there's nothing to install and no account data leaves your device.
</Note>
