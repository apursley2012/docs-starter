PunctuOwlity is a lightweight, browser-based event tracking app that helps you stay on top of every important date. Whether you want to remember a birthday, keep tabs on an upcoming appointment, or plan a trip, PunctuOwlity gives you a clean, focused space to log and manage your events — all without downloading anything or creating a cloud account.

## How it works

Using PunctuOwlity follows a simple, linear flow:

1. **Sign up** — Create a local account with your name, email, username, and password.
2. **Set your SMS preference** — Choose whether to allow SMS alerts for upcoming events.
3. **Reach your events dashboard** — Your event cards are waiting for you the moment you sign in.
4. **Add events** — Tap the **＋** floating action button (FAB) to create a new event with a title, date, time, and optional reminder.
5. **Get reminders** — On the day of any event with a reminder enabled, PunctuOwlity delivers a browser notification so you never miss it.

## Your data stays with you

PunctuOwlity saves everything — your account details and all your events — directly in your browser's **localStorage**. No data is ever transmitted to a server or stored in the cloud. This means:

- Your information never leaves your device.
- You don't need to trust a third party with your personal details.
- Your events are always available offline, as long as you open PunctuOwlity in the same browser on the same device.

The trade-off is that localStorage is tied to a specific browser on a specific device. If you clear your browser data or switch to a different browser or device, your events and account will not carry over.

<Note>
  PunctuOwlity works best in modern browsers. Use the latest version of **Chrome**, **Safari**, **Firefox**, or **Edge** for full compatibility with localStorage and browser notifications.
</Note>
