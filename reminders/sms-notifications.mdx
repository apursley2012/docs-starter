Right after you create your PunctuOwlity account, the app shows you a one-time notification preference screen before taking you to your events. The choice you make here controls whether PunctuOwlity can send you browser notifications for events with reminders enabled — so it is worth understanding what each option does.

## The notification preference screen

The preference screen appears **once**, immediately after you sign up and before you see your events calendar for the first time. It will not appear again on subsequent logins.

The screen presents two options:

- **Allow SMS Notifications** — a turquoise button that opts you in to event notifications
- **No, thanks** — a coral button that opts you out

Your choice is saved to your browser's local storage and does not expire. PunctuOwlity reads it every time you open the app to decide whether to request notification permission.

## What each choice does

### Allow SMS Notifications

Choosing this option tells PunctuOwlity you want to receive event notifications. The app will:

1. Save your preference in your browser's local storage.
2. Prompt your browser to grant notification permission (your browser may show its own confirmation dialog).
3. Fire a browser pop-up notification on the day of any event that has its **Reminder** toggle switched on.

### No, thanks

Choosing this option tells PunctuOwlity to skip notifications entirely. The app will:

1. Save your preference in your browser's local storage.
2. Skip the browser notification permission prompt.
3. Never fire pop-up notifications, even for events with the Reminder toggle on.

Your event cards will still display the green alarm-on or red alarm-off icon to show each event's reminder state — but those icons will be visual-only, and no notifications will fire.

<Note>
  PunctuOwlity is a static web app and cannot send real SMS text messages. No backend or SMS credentials are involved. The **Allow SMS Notifications** label reflects the feature's original mobile design. All notifications in the web app are delivered as browser pop-up notifications through the browser's built-in Notification API.
</Note>

## Changing your preference

Your SMS preference is stored in your browser's local storage for `apursley2012.github.io`. There is no in-app settings screen to change it after signup.

To reset your preference, clear your browser's site data for `apursley2012.github.io`. After clearing site data, you will be taken through the signup flow again, including the notification preference screen.

<Warning>
  Clearing site data for apursley2012.github.io removes **all** locally stored information — including your events, your account credentials, and your notification preference. You will need to create a new account and re-enter your events after clearing site data.
</Warning>
