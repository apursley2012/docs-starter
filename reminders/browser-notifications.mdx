PunctuOwlity delivers event reminders as browser pop-up notifications — the same kind your operating system surfaces for email, calendar apps, and other services. When conditions are right, a notification appears on your screen showing the event title and time, so you can act on it without switching back to the app.

## How browser notifications work

PunctuOwlity uses the browser's built-in **Notification API** to display event reminders without requiring any installed software or native app.

- **Permission follows your preference.** If you chose **Allow SMS Notifications** during the one-time setup after signup, PunctuOwlity requests your browser's notification permission as soon as you reach the events screen. Your browser may show its own confirmation dialog — you need to allow it there as well.
- **Notifications appear at the system level.** Once permission is granted, reminders surface as standard system notifications — the same kind you see from any other app on your device. They appear even if PunctuOwlity is not your active tab.
- **Same-day delivery only.** PunctuOwlity checks today's date each time you open the app and fires notifications only for events scheduled on the current date.
- **Once per session.** Each notification fires at most once per browser session. If you reload the page or navigate away and return, you will not see the same notification again until you start a new browser session.

## What triggers a notification

All four of the following conditions must be true for a notification to fire:

1. **The event's date matches today's date.** PunctuOwlity compares each event's stored date against the current date when the app loads.
2. **The event has its Reminder toggle switched on.** Events with the red alarm-off icon will not trigger a notification, even if all other conditions are met.
3. **You have allowed notifications in your browser.** Both your PunctuOwlity SMS preference must be set to **Allow SMS Notifications** *and* your browser must have granted notification permission for `apursley2012.github.io`.
4. **You have not already seen the notification in this session.** PunctuOwlity records each fired notification in session storage. If the notification has already appeared during your current session, it will not repeat.

## Managing notification permissions

If you need to review or change the notification permission for PunctuOwlity, use your browser's settings.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Chrome">
    1. Open Chrome and select the three-dot menu in the top-right corner.
    2. Go to **Settings** → **Privacy and security** → **Site settings**.
    3. Under **Permissions**, select **Notifications**.
    4. Find `apursley2012.github.io` in the **Allowed** or **Not allowed** list.
    5. Click the entry to change the permission to **Allow** or **Block**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Firefox">
    1. Open Firefox and select the hamburger menu in the top-right corner.
    2. Go to **Settings** → **Privacy & Security**.
    3. Scroll to the **Permissions** section and click **Settings** next to **Notifications**.
    4. Find `apursley2012.github.io` in the list.
    5. Use the **Status** dropdown to change the permission to **Allow** or **Block**, then click **Save Changes**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Safari">
    1. Navigate to [apursley2012.github.io/punctuowlity](https://apursley2012.github.io/punctuowlity) in Safari.
    2. Open the **Safari** menu in the menu bar.
    3. Select **Settings for This Website**.
    4. Find **Notifications** in the panel and set it to **Allow** or **Deny**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  If PunctuOwlity has notification permission in your browser but you still do not see any pop-ups, check your device's system-level notification settings. On macOS, go to **System Settings → Notifications** and confirm that your browser is allowed to send notifications. On Windows, check **Settings → System → Notifications** and make sure notifications are enabled for your browser.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Notifications only fire while PunctuOwlity is open in a browser tab. The app does not run in the background and cannot send notifications after you close the tab or quit your browser.
</Note>
