PunctuOwlity requires no installation and no cloud account — you can go from zero to tracking events in under two minutes. This guide walks you through opening the app, creating your account, setting notification preferences, and adding your very first event.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open PunctuOwlity">
    Navigate to [https://apursley2012.github.io/punctuowlity](https://apursley2012.github.io/punctuowlity) in your browser. The login page loads immediately — no install, no app store, no waiting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Click **Sign Up** beneath the login form, then fill in the following fields:

    | Field | Required |
    |---|---|
    | First Name | Yes |
    | Last Name | Yes |
    | Email Address | Yes |
    | Phone (for SMS alerts) | No |
    | Username | Yes |
    | Password | Yes |
    | Confirm Password | Yes |

    When every required field is complete, click **Sign Up**. PunctuOwlity saves your account locally in your browser and redirects you to the login page. Sign in with your new credentials to continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your notification preferences">
    After your first login, PunctuOwlity asks whether you want to receive SMS alerts for upcoming events. Choose one of the two options:

    - **Allow SMS Notifications** — enables SMS alerts so PunctuOwlity can notify you on the day of a reminder event.
    - **No, thanks** — skips SMS alerts; you can still add and view events normally.

    You must make a selection before you can access the events dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your first event">
    On the events dashboard, tap the **＋** floating action button (FAB) in the bottom-right corner of the screen to open the Add Event form. Fill in the details:

    1. **Event Title** — give your event a clear, recognisable name.
    2. **Date** — pick the date the event occurs.
    3. **Time** — enter the time the event occurs.
    4. **Reminder** — toggle this on if you want a browser notification on the day of the event.

    Click **Save Event** to add it to your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See your event on the dashboard">
    Your new event appears as a card on the dashboard. Each card displays:

    - The **weekday abbreviation** (e.g. MON, SAT) at the top
    - The **day number** in large text
    - The **event title**
    - The **time** (or ALL DAY)
    - An **alarm icon** in the top-right corner — filled when a reminder is active, outlined when it is off

    Use the category tabs at the top of the dashboard to filter events by All Events, Birthdays, Appointments, or Trips.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can edit or delete any event using the icons in the top-right corner of its card — the pencil icon opens the edit form, and the bin icon removes the event immediately.
</Tip>
